Product judgement

How I think
about products

A shipped interface is only one part of the work. I use five questions to decide whether an idea solves a real problem, deserves more engineering, and has a credible next experiment.

01

User problem

Who is making the decision, what job are they trying to finish, and where does the current experience break down?

02

Alternatives

What can people use today — a spreadsheet, official portal, notes app, manual service, or simply doing nothing?

03

Product trade-offs

Why is a lightweight, local-first, or account-free approach appropriate, and which limitations does that choice introduce?

04

Commercial path

How could the product earn attention, feedback, qualified enquiries, repeat use, or another meaningful conversion?

05

Validation

What evidence should exist next — rule fixtures, interviews, task completion, supplier quotes, or a small demand test?
