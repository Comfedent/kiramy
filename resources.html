How products are selected

Recommendations start with a job to be done, not a commission rate. A tool has to solve a practical workflow problem before it belongs in a guide.

01 / Fit

Workflow first

Does the product remove a real step from capture, decision, execution or review?

02 / Trust

Clear limits

Does it explain privacy, exports, storage, pricing and the situations where it is not a good fit?

03 / Cost

Total effort

Does the monthly price still make sense after setup time, learning cost and migration work?

Starter guides

These guide briefs are the first content lanes for affiliate applications and partner outreach. They match the existing Kira focus on useful local tools, lightweight workflows and honest validation.

AI meeting notes for small teams

Before choosing a meeting recorder, compare how well it turns a conversation into decisions, owners and follow-up work.

  • Check platform support, consent controls and export options.
  • Score summary quality by action accuracy, not transcript length.
  • Test with sales calls, internal planning and client updates before paying annually.

Remote work desk setup

A useful desk setup improves calls, posture, focus and cable friction. Everything else is optional.

  • Buy audio and lighting before upgrading camera quality.
  • Pick a monitor based on the apps kept open every day.
  • Avoid decorative gear that makes the workspace harder to reset.

AI and productivity software stack

A lightweight stack should help one person or a small team write, plan, decide and ship without turning into another dashboard habit.

  • Choose tools that connect to existing work, not separate silos.
  • Prefer exportable data and clear pricing limits.
  • Review adoption after one week, not after the annual bill renews.
